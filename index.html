Soul Food · Sat 31 October

Some afternoons are for lying low. This is not one of them.

Soul Food is a long, loud, feel good Saturday. Grab your friends, lock in your spot and spend the afternoon on a welcome cocktail, bottomless drinks, a delicious set menu and the R&B anthems you have been singing in the car for twenty years.

Birthday, girls' day out, someone's leaving Bangkok, someone just got promoted, or nobody needs a reason at all. Gather the crew and come through.

Coming solo? Even better. This is the kind of afternoon where you turn up knowing nobody and leave with a few new best friends saved in your phone. Everyone in the room is here for the same songs, so you are never really on your own.

✦

Groups and Tables

Soul Food is built for groups, but solo guests are always welcome. Capacity is limited and bookings are essential.

  • Solo guests: book a single ticket through the ticket link.
  • Groups of 2 to 8: please book single tickets together in one transaction directly through the ticket link
  • Groups of 9 or more: email hello@bkkdayparties.com or DM us on Instagram and our team will arrange your booking and send you a private booking link

Can't see the group size you want, or it shows as sold out? Message us before buying single tickets. We will do our best to fit you in.

Joining a friend who has already booked? Email us with the name on the booking and we will do our best to add you to their booking. Please note, this is not guaranteed as table and seating capacity is limited.

Dress Code

Come comfortable, come looking good. Think nice casual. It's Bangkok, so dress for the heat, just leave the sportswear, gym gear and flip flops at home.

Some editions will have a theme. Dressing to the theme is never essential, but always encouraged. It makes the day (and the photos) better.

October: All White Halloween. Heavenly or haunted, your call. Just keep it white.

FAQs

Is this a seated event?+
The Soul Session (2:00pm to 4:30pm) is seated. From 4:30pm it becomes a standing party.
Can I come for the after party only?+
Yes. After party tickets are THB 550 presale or THB 700 at the door, and include a welcome drink. Doors open at 4:30pm.
Can I come on my own?+
Absolutely. Plenty of people come solo to our events. It is a friendly, sing along crowd and you will leave with a few new names in your phone.
Is the food set menu?+
Yes. Every Soul Session guest gets the Soul Food set menu. Vegetarian option included. If you have any specific allergies, please notify us ahead of the event so we can cater to you.
What does bottomless cover?+
The bottomless package runs for 2 hours and covers Mya's Mimosa, Destiny's Punch and soft drinks. The full bar menu is also available for purchase throughout the event.
Can packages be split?+
No. Packages are per person and cannot be shared or split for a lower price.
Is there an age limit?+
Yes. 20+ only. ID may be checked at the door.
How do I choose my meal?+
Once your tickets are ordered, complete your menu on the menu selection page. Choose a starter, appetiser and main for each guest.
Can I change my menu choice?+
Yes, until the deadline. Submit the form again with the same ticket number and we will use your latest choice.
When will I get my tickets?+
Your tickets are emailed to you straight away after payment.
Can I get a refund?+

All ticket sales are final. We are unable to offer refunds for a change of mind or if you can no longer attend.

If we cancel the event, your ticket will be moved to the next event date. If you can't make the new date, let us know and we will refund your ticket price. Booking fees are non-refundable.

Refunds can take up to 14 business days to process and will be paid back to your original payment method.

Capacity Is Limited

Capacity is limited and bookings are essential, so don't hit snooze. Round up your crew, or just bring yourself, and lock in your Saturday.
